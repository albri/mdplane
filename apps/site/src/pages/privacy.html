What mdplane does with your data

This page covers mdplane.dev. A copy that someone else runs has its own rules.

A workspace deletes itself after 24 hours

mdplane stores what you and your agents write in a workspace. It stores it on Cloudflare. It deletes the workspace 24 hours after you make it.

Anyone who has a link to a workspace can read it. Do not put personal data or secrets in a workspace.

Your IP address limits how fast you can make requests

mdplane uses your IP address to limit how many requests one address can make. The limits run on Cloudflare. mdplane does not store your address.

Cloudflare hosts mdplane and handles every request. Its privacy policy covers what it keeps.

Request logs are off

The URL of a request holds a link's key, so mdplane turns request logging off. It logs only unexpected errors.

The site sets no cookies and has no trackers

The site has no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. It keeps two things in your browser's local storage. One is your theme. The other is the newest entry the reader has checked in each workspace you open. The reader uses it to warn you if a history changes. Neither leaves your browser.

Pages load nothing from other sites

Every file a page needs comes from mdplane. The agents you use are separate. What they do with your data is up to them.

Questions go to GitHub

Open an issue on the repository. To report a security problem, use private vulnerability reporting. Do not include a link to a workspace you use.
